# Report Pages

## Overview
The report is organized into subject-based pages to support both executive monitoring and detailed analytical exploration.

## Page Structure

### Home
Purpose:
- provide report entry point
- guide navigation
- introduce the solution layout

### Overview
Purpose:
- present executive KPIs
- summarize revenue and high-level business performance
- show key directional metrics and trends

Typical content:
- KPI cards
- high-level revenue trends
- contribution visuals
- summary-level business insights

### Sales
Purpose:
- analyze revenue trends and order performance
- review sales distribution and status outcomes
- support month-level and category-level exploration

Typical content:
- net revenue trends
- MoM comparisons
- channel breakdown
- order status analysis
- daily / monthly trend visuals

### Products
Purpose:
- analyze product and category contribution
- review product-level revenue and profitability
- identify top and bottom performers

### Customers
Purpose:
- analyze customer acquisition and repeat behavior
- review customer growth and segmentation patterns
- evaluate retention-oriented metrics

### Details
Purpose:
- provide deeper financial and operational analysis
- support detailed variance and comparison review
- expose supporting breakdowns behind headline KPIs

### Info / Guide Pages
Purpose:
- explain how to use the report
- provide KPI guidance
- explain interactions such as filters, reset actions, and navigation

## Navigation Design
The report uses guided navigation patterns to support:

- page switching
- context-specific help
- clean interaction flow
- user-friendly exploration without overcrowding visuals
